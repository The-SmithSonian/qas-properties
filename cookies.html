1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are commonly used to make sites work, remember preferences, or gather usage statistics.

2. Cookies on this site

This site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. However, some embedded content may set cookies of its own:

  • Google Maps — used in our Contact section to show our office location.
  • YouTube — used to play property videos, where included.
  • Google Fonts — used to load the typefaces on this site.

These are provided by Google and are governed by Google's own privacy and cookie policies, not ours.

3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser's settings. Blocking cookies from Google Maps or YouTube may prevent the map or videos from displaying correctly.
