1. Who we are

QAS Properties Nigeria Limited ("we", "us") is a real estate development and sales company based at No 5 Kwaji Close, Maitama, Abuja FCT. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

When you use our "Book an inspection" form, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address (if given), the property you are interested in, a preferred date, and any message you write. We do not require you to create an account to browse listings.

3. How we use your information

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange property inspections, and follow up about properties you have expressed interest in. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

4. Where your information is stored

Enquiry details are stored securely in our database, hosted by Supabase, and are only accessible to authorized staff of QAS Properties Nigeria Limited.

5. Third-party services

This site embeds Google Maps and may embed YouTube videos, and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These third parties may set their own cookies or collect technical data (such as your IP address) when their content loads, under their own privacy policies.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us using the details below.
